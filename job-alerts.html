FRESH JOB ALERTS

Tell JobSeek what vacancies you want.

Your preferences are stored in your JobSeek account. The notification robot checks published vacancies against your preferences and saved CV compatibility, while the admin panel shows the exact request so the team can notify you manually.

How it works

  1. You choose the roles, locations, job types and notification channels.
  2. If you have saved a CV, JobSeek can also compare new published vacancies against it.
  3. When a match is detected, the notification robot records the vacancy for the JobSeek admin.
  4. The admin can review the original vacancy and contact you using your chosen channel.
