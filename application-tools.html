JOBSEEK APPLICATION PACK

Everything you need to apply professionally.

Choose a vacancy, review the employer's exact requirements, then prepare truthful documents and a clear submission plan. JobSeek helps you prepare; the employer makes the hiring decision.

01Professional CVContact details, summary, skills, experience, education, projects, certifications and professional links. Keep it truthful, clear and ATS-friendly.02Cover LetterTailor the opening, relevant evidence, company/role connection and professional closing to the specific vacancy.
03Application EmailSubject line, greeting, role reference, short evidence-based message, attachment list and professional signature.
04Document ChecklistCV, cover letter, application form, portfolio/work samples, certificates or licences, references and right-to-work documents only when requested.
05Interview PreparationPrepare examples from your real experience, understand the employer and role, prepare questions, and check interview logistics.
06Application TrackerRecord employer, role, application date, source, deadline, status, interview dates, follow-up dates and notes.

Before you submit

✓ Read the vacancy requirements again.

✓ Make sure your CV matches your real experience.

✓ Check names, dates, phone number, email and links.

✓ Make sure the cover letter names the correct role and employer.

✓ Attach every document the employer actually requests.

✓ Use the employer's official application route whenever available.

✓ Never pay an individual for a job offer without independently verifying the employer and recruitment process.

✓ Keep a copy of exactly what you submitted.
