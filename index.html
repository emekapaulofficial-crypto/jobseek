WHY JOBSEEK WAS CREATED

A journey built to create opportunity beyond borders.

JobSeek was created from a simple idea: useful opportunities should be easier to discover, understand and act on — whether someone is starting from Nigeria or searching for opportunities anywhere in the world.

01Connect people to real opportunitiesBring job seekers closer to current vacancies and the original employer application website, so people can spend less time searching and more time applying.
02Open access to global opportunitiesMake it easier for people to discover work and career opportunities across countries, including roles that may offer remote work or international pathways.
03Help first-time job seekers apply betterGive people practical tools to understand vacancy requirements, compare their CV with a role and prepare stronger application materials without inventing qualifications.
04Build technology that creates wider impactUse digital products and entrepreneurship to connect people, information and opportunities — starting from Nigeria and designed to serve a global community.

The bigger vision

The goal is not only to create another job website. It is to build a growing ecosystem of practical tools and businesses that can help people find opportunities, prepare themselves and take meaningful steps toward a better future. Through JobSeek, Global Travel Agency and other ventures, Mr. Igwe Emeka Paul is building from Nigeria with a wider global audience in mind.
