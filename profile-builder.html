Recommended JobSeek flow

  1. Open a vacancy and read the original employer requirements.
  2. Copy the requirements into JobSeek or use the vacancy data already supplied.
  3. Generate your tailored CV and cover letter using only truthful information.
  4. JobSeek generates LinkedIn and Indeed copy-ready sections from that application.
  5. Open LinkedIn or Indeed and paste the information into the fields they request.
  6. Review everything before saving or submitting.
